# Data Collection Instrument

## Annex 02 — Solid Waste Management Questionnaire

### Research Title

**“Intelligent System with QR Codes and Artificial Intelligence for the Optimization of Solid Waste Management in Amarilis, Huánuco – 2026”**

### Data Collection Technique

**Survey**

### Instrument

**Structured Questionnaire**

### Target Population

Families participating in the **Source Separation and Selective Waste Collection Program** in Amarilis, Huánuco.

### Instrument Objective

The objective of this questionnaire is to collect information about family participation in solid waste management and to evaluate the influence of the intelligent system based on Quick Response Code (QR) and Artificial Intelligence on the registration, monitoring, and control of the source separation and selective waste collection process.

---

## Data Analysis and Reproducibility

The questionnaire responses are structured as research data and can be analyzed using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib. The analysis supports the descriptive and comparative evaluation of the two research variables and their dimensions.

The corresponding analytical notebook is maintained in the `05_pipeline` directory to support reproducibility and transparent analysis.
