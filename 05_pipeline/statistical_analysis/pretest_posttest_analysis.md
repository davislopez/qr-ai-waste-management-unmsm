# Pretest and Posttest Analysis

The research follows a quasi-experimental pretest-posttest design.

The pretest measures the study variables before implementation of the intelligent system.

The posttest measures the same variables after implementation.

The analysis compares the measurements obtained at both stages to identify changes associated with the implementation of the intelligent system based on QR Codes and Artificial Intelligence.

The statistical test used for the final comparison will be determined according to the characteristics and distribution of the collected data.
