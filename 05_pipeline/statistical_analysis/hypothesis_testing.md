# Hypothesis Testing

The research includes a general hypothesis and specific hypotheses related to the improvement of solid waste management.

The hypotheses address:

1. Family participation
2. Amount of segregated waste
3. Participation frequency
4. The Artificial Intelligence component

The statistical analysis will be conducted using the actual research data.

The final inferential test, significance level, test statistic, p-value, and decision regarding the null and alternative hypotheses will be reported after the data have been analyzed.

No statistical result will be reported before the corresponding analysis has been performed.
